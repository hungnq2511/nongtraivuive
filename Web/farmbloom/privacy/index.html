Privacy Policy

Farm Bloom: Sunny Acres (iOS) · Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Farm Bloom: Sunny Acres (the “Game”, “we”, “us”), developed and published by Nguyen Quang Hung, handles information when you download and play the Game on iPhone. We built the Game to collect as little information as possible. If you have any questions, contact us at founder@nutrica.site.

1. Summary

  • No account, login or registration is required.
  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved locally on your device.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded ads, provided by Unity LevelPlay and partner ad networks. These partners may collect device and usage data as described below.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your farm, items, level, settings and language preference are stored only on your device. This data is not sent to our servers (the Game does not operate any servers). It is deleted when you use Settings › Delete data & restart in the Game or uninstall the Game.

3. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs and a Starter Pack) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment under the Apple Privacy Policy. The Game only receives a transaction identifier and product identifier so it can deliver your items and support “Restore Purchases”. This information stays on your device.

4. Advertising

The Game displays rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, to receive bonus gems or to finish a timer). Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its partner network Unity Ads. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may automatically collect:

DataExamplesPurpose
Device identifiersIdentifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), Identifier for Vendors (IDFV)Serving and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Device and network informationDevice model, OS version, language, time zone, IP address and coarse (approximate) location derived from itServing relevant ads, analytics
Ad interaction dataAds viewed, completed or tappedAd measurement, rewarding you, analytics
DiagnosticsCrash and performance data of the ad SDKFixing errors and improving performance

This data is not linked to your name or any account. Our partners process it under their own privacy policies, including the Unity Game Player & App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices (App Tracking Transparency)

Before ads are initialized, iOS asks whether you allow the Game to track your activity. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Declining tracking or never watching ads does not limit your ability to play the Game.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when crops are ready to harvest). No push service or server is used and no data leaves your device. You can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings.

6. Children

The Game is rated 4+ but is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their policies; you can contact them or us to exercise your rights. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests can be fulfilled by deleting the Game.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of the “sharing” of personal data for targeted advertising. You can opt out of tracking with the iOS setting described above, and you may contact us at founder@nutrica.site with any request. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We do not operate servers that store your data. Data handled by Apple and our advertising partners is protected by their security measures and transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
