Frequently asked questions

I bought gems or the Starter Pack but did not receive them.

Make sure you are connected to the internet, then open the Gem Shop (tap the gem counter at the top of the screen) and tap Restore Purchases. If the items still do not appear, email us with your Apple order ID (found in the purchase receipt email from Apple) and we will help.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. The game can be played offline; an internet connection is only needed for purchases and optional ads.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Your farm is saved on your device. It is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer backup), so restoring a backup to a new iPhone restores your farm.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear icon (top right) › Language, then choose English or Tiếng Việt.

How do I stop personalized ads?

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one. To limit tracking, go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn it off for Farm Bloom. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my game data?

In the game, open Settings › Delete data & restart, or uninstall the app.
